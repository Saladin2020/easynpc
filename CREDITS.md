# Credits

## Art

- `samples/maps/multicultural_village.*` — the Easy Tilemap sample village (CC0), exported with
  `easytilemap export` and rendered with `easytilemap render --no-characters` (day) and `--night`.
- `samples/characters/town_*.png` — Easy Sprite top-down character sheets shipped with Easy Tilemap (CC0).

## Writing

- NPCs, places, schedules, `samples/dialogues/*.ezd` and `samples/quests/*.ezq` are written for Easy Suite. Thai names
  are in Thai script; Malay-Muslim neighbours only like halal food; no sacred figures, scripture or recitation appear.
